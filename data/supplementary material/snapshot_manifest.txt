Supplementary Material Snapshot Manifest
========================================

Purpose
-------
This manifest documents the article-facing supplementary files stored under
`data/supplementary material/`. The package supports verification of aggregate
manuscript counts, supplementary tables, dictionary inputs, and validation
summaries without redistributing licensed raw Web of Science Core Collection
exports.

Manuscript title
----------------
`A reproducible bibliographic landscape of AI in oncology`

- Records after deduplication and 2026 exclusion: `59,828`
